Logo

✓ Do
  • Use the logo on approved background colors only
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the logomark on all sides
  • Use the full-color logo whenever possible
  • Use the white version on dark or brand-color backgrounds
  • Download fresh assets from this guide
✗ Don't
  • Stretch, skew, or distort the logo in any direction
  • Change the logo colors or apply gradients
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or other effects
  • Place the logo on busy photos or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Recreate the logo using the typeface manually
02 — Identity

Colors

CultureFly's color system is intentionally neutral at its core. Black and white carry everything — across every property, genre, and surface. Signal Green exists as a rare, high-value signal. Everything else is contextual.

Tier 1 — Core
Black & White. Always present. Never replaced.
→
Tier 2 — Brand Accent
Signal Green. Reserved for rare, intentional brand moments only.
→
Tier 3 — Contextual
Per-property or per-section. Defined case by case.

Tier 2 — Brand Accent

Signal Green is present in the brand, but almost never visible. Reserve it for moments of genuine emphasis — a single interactive state, a launch badge, a critical alert. If you're asking yourself whether to use it, the answer is probably no.

Tier 3 — Contextual Colors

Contextual colors are assigned per section, genre, or licensed property. They replace the Tier 1 palette's supporting role — not its foundation. Black, white, and Signal Green always remain present. Contextual colors should be approved by Brand before use.

✓ Contextual colors may be used for
Section backgrounds and borders, genre or property labels, packaging accent lines, iconography, illustration fills, and hover/active states specific to that section.
✗ Contextual colors must never replace
The primary Black/White foundation, the Signal Green brand accent, logo colors, or body copy color. They are additive — not substitutive.
